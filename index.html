Research Overview

My doctoral research focuses on strategic infrastructure management planning under uncertainty using agent-based modelling. The central objective is to develop and apply surrogate models that enable faster mobility simulations, thereby supporting large-scale scenario and population analysis and the exploration of uncertainties in (im)possible future mobility scenarios. I conduct my research within the FUTURES project, which is part of the ETH Mobility Initiative and supported by SBB. I am based at the Chair of Infrastructure Management at ETH Zürich and am supervised by Prof. Dr. Bryan T. Adey.

Publications

Preprint · 2025
Hierarchical Strategic Decision-Making in Layered Mobility Systems
Mingjia He, Zhiyu He, Jan Ghadamian, Florian Dörfler, Emilio Frazzoli, Gioele Zardini

arXiv:2511.08734 [eess.SY], 2025. — Urban mobility is modelled as a tri-level Stackelberg game among travelers, operators, and the municipality. A gradient-free feedback optimisation scheme allows the municipality to iteratively update taxes, subsidies, and constraints without an explicit system model, while lower levels respond through traveler and operator equilibrium computations.

Journal Article · 2022
Optical feedback control loop for the precise and robust acoustic focusing of cells, micro- and nanoparticles
Cooper L. Harshbarger, Michael S. Gerlt, Jan A. Ghadamian, Davide C. Bernardoni, Jess G. Snedeker, Jürg Dual

Lab on a Chip, 22, 2810–2819, 2022. DOI: 10.1039/D2LC00376G. — An optical feedback control loop autonomously optimises the acoustic excitation frequency in microfluidic devices, outperforming experienced operators in focusing 5 µm and 1 µm polystyrene particles and achieving focusing of 600 nm particles previously considered beyond reach.
